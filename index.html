Public-opinion research

Better public insight starts with better participation.

Daylitica builds engaging daily experiences that help people share what they think, test what they assume about others, and return often enough to create richer understanding over time. Our first product is PollShot — a short daily game of opinion, perception, prediction, and discovery.

Meet PollShot About Daylitica

The idea

Public opinion shouldn't feel like paperwork.

Traditional surveys ask people to stop what they're doing, fill out a form, and disappear. We take a different approach: participation that's brief, rewarding, transparent, and worth coming back to.

PollShot puts that approach into practice, one day at a time.

The method

One question at a time. More context over time.

Daylitica studies how opinions, perceptions, confidence, context, and change relate over time — while respecting the people providing the answers.

We're early, and meaningful public insight depends on a broad, representative community. We're clear about what our data can and can't support at every stage.

The build

Designed for discovery, built for rigor.

PollShot makes the experience enjoyable. Behind it, Daylitica handles the harder part: question design, recurring measurement, contextual analysis, geographic insight, and clear explanations of methodology and limitations.

How we work

Trust is the product.

Nonpartisan

We measure; we don't advocate. Neutral questions, results reported without a side or a spin.

Privacy-first

We work in aggregate. Your answers help draw the big picture — we never sell, or need, your identity to do it.

Rigorous

Grounded in neutral questions, aggregate reporting, and clear limits — insight that grows more representative as the community does.
