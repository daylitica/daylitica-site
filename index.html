What we do

Most of what we believe about each other, we assume.

Media and feeds tell us what "the other side" thinks; we rarely ask. Daylitica asks — at scale, and over time — then shows the answer. The result is a clearer, quieter picture of how people and communities actually see things, and how that view shifts month over month, year over year.

We're a research company, not a platform for opinions. Every question is written to be neutral, and every result is reported straight.

Our first product

PollShot

A daily app: answer a few quick questions, guess how everyone else answered, and see how close you were. A small, fun habit that adds up to something larger — an honest, living read on what people think.
