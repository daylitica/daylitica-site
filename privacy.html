Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Applies to Daylitica, LLC and the PollShot app.

The short version.

  • We work with aggregate answers — the big picture, not the individual.
  • We never sell your identity.
  • Signing in is required, but we keep who you are separate from how we report the research.
  • We aim to limit collection to information relevant to operating and improving PollShot.

Who we are

Daylitica, LLC ("Daylitica," "we") is a public-opinion research company. PollShot is our app, where you answer short daily questions, guess how others answered, and see how you compare. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Your responses

The answers you give — your opinions, your guesses about what others think, and light self-descriptions ("morning person or night owl?"). These are the heart of the research.

Your account

To use PollShot you sign in with Apple. We receive an identifier for your account and the information Apple shares with us, which may include your name and an email address (or Apple's private relay address). Within the research, your answers are keyed to a random internal ID kept separate from this sign-in information.

Details you share during setup

PollShot asks for an approximate area (such as your country, state, or metro area) and your year of birth. The area is self-provided and approximate — never precise location, GPS, or your address — and you can skip it. These help us group and compare answers.

Device and usage data

How you interact with the app (timings, taps, gestures), basic device information, and your device's IP address.

How we use your data

  • To produce aggregate insight — how people and communities think, and how that shifts over time. This is our research and, in aggregate, may be shared or licensed.
  • To power the experience you see — your comparison to others, your history, your streak.
  • To operate PollShot, keep it secure, prevent abuse, and improve it.

Keeping identity separate

We hold your sign-in identity apart from your answers, so the research doesn't run on who you are. When we report or share results, we describe groups and places, not people. If we ever run sponsored questions, they are clearly labeled, and sponsors receive aggregate results only. We do not provide research clients or sponsors with names, contact information, or account identifiers.

Selling your data

We don't sell your personal information.

When we share

  • Aggregate, de-identified insight — the product of the research, describing groups and places, not people.
  • Service providers process data for us under contractual privacy and security obligations.
  • Legal requirements — if compelled by law, to the extent required.
  • In a business transfer (such as a merger), with this policy's protections carried forward.

Age

PollShot is intended for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your choices and privacy rights

You may skip optional questions and setup details.

You can delete your account from within PollShot. When you do, we delete the personal information associated with your account, except where we need to retain limited information for legal or security reasons. Aggregate or de-identified research that is no longer linked to your account may be retained.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, such as the right to access or correct your personal information. To ask a question or exercise an applicable right, email privacy@daylitica.com.

Security & retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your personal information. We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including security and legal purposes.

Changes

We may update this policy as PollShot grows. We'll post the new version here and update the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@daylitica.com.
